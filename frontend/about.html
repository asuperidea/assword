How Assword Works

Assword was built by Simon Crystal for Hackclub Cloudfall

Where Passwords are Stored

All passwords are encrypted and stored in a SQL database on my personal Mac Mini. Because passwords are encrypted I have no way to view your sensitive data.

Fully Encrypted

Your passwords are encrypted in-browser, only an IV and cipher text is sent to the database. Only you can unencrypt them.

No One Knows

Attackers cannot figure out which emails do/don't have accounts.
